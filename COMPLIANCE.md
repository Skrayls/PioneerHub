# Compliance and policy baseline

Not legal advice. Scope is deliberately chosen to avoid regulated activities at launch.

## Pi policy conclusions (verified 2026-08-15)

- Pi trademark may be referenced truthfully, but `Pi` in an app name, domain or social handle needs a trademark licence. Do not use Pi brand colours or imply affiliation.
- Pi apps needing login/payments must use Pi SDK; apps must never request passphrases. Current official app guidelines require a privacy policy and data minimization.
- Referral Team membership is fixed at sign-up; there is no legitimate team-transfer product.

## EU scope

MVP has no custody, exchange, brokerage, conversion or transfers on behalf of users. Any later functionality near those activities pauses for specialist legal review under MiCA. Accounts, UGC, listings, marketing or payments require prior GDPR/consumer/DSA implementation appropriate to the feature.
