# Merchant Readiness Desk V1

## Product decision

**User problem:** a merchant interested in Pi has no practical way to assess operational readiness without prematurely accepting a payment.

**User job:** choose an operating scenario, identify the most relevant gaps, and leave with an ordered, safe readiness plan.

**Interaction:** a browser-only scenario selection followed by a four-area operational assessment: fulfilment, refunds, customer communication, and fraud/escalation.

**Output:** a readiness level, the gaps that need attention, an ordered next-action plan, and a safe link to relevant PioneerHub safety material.

**Repeat-use loop:** rerun the desk when a fulfilment method, refund policy, customer support process, or fraud-handling process changes.

**Differentiation:** this is an operating workflow, not another Learn article, payment prompt, wallet integration, or generic merchant checklist.

**Whitepaper contribution:** expands PioneerHub from individual safety/discovery toward practical ecosystem readiness for a second real user segment.

**Maintenance burden:** bounded local decision rules and links to existing safety content; no feed, backend, user record, API, or merchant directory.

**Future economic path:** if demonstrated demand exists, a later owner-approved product could offer merchant education or verification services; V1 makes no commercial offer and accepts no payment.

## Safety and preflight

`HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED=false`.

V1 uses no accounts, personal-data storage, user database, email/notifications, external API, secret, Cloudflare binding, DNS change, Pi Developer Portal action, wallet collection, payment, Mainnet capability, paid service, legal/commercial commitment, or financial advice. Inputs remain in the current page only. A semantic useful-action event may be emitted without identifiers, profiles, cookies, or persistent analytics.

## Limitations

The desk does not verify a merchant, assess legal/tax obligations, recommend accepting Pi, integrate a wallet, simulate a payment, or provide financial advice. A completed result means only that the selected operating considerations were reviewed.
