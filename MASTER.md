- Marketplace, Mainnet payments, regulated activity, public high-risk statements, paid commitments, domain purchase, KYC and legal acceptance require the appropriate founder gate.
- Work autonomously on reversible, low-risk tasks. Preserve decisions, risks, metrics and project state in this repository.
