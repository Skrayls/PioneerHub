# Monetization architecture

## Sequence

Free safety and learning → measured use → useful Testnet experience → one Mainnet-compatible, paid utility only after approval and policy re-check.

## Future Pioneer Pass hypothesis

Potential benefits: advanced App Radar filters, beta access, deeper practical guides, member tools and partner benefits. It cannot be merely a payment test, hype access or price speculation. Price is deliberately unset.

## Revenue ledger

When the first payment is authorized, record: date, product, Pi received, Pi spent, Pi retained, counterparty class, cost, fee, fiat-equivalent reference (if any), and evidence reference. Never publish user transaction details.
