## Selected first paid utility

**Guided Pi scam check plus evidence pack**

Why it wins:

- closest to PioneerHub's core trust promise,
- clearly useful without speculation,
- low marginal delivery cost,
- can be sold as a focused, digital, repeatable utility,
- does not require hiding essential safety advice behind a paywall.

## Free vs paid boundary

Always free:

- passphrase safety
- basic scam warnings
- essential wallet/KYC/Mainnet guidance

Potentially paid later:

- deeper personalized scam evidence review,
- structured case triage,
- exportable checklist/report bundle,
- merchant/vendor risk worksheet.

## First-100-Pi path

1. Prove Safety Center repeat use.
2. Identify the most repeated trust questions.
3. Package the paid layer around time-saving and higher-confidence review, not around fear.
4. Launch only after Testnet learning flow and Pi policy re-check.

## Revenue ledger

When the first payment is authorized, record: date, product, Pi received, Pi spent, Pi retained, counterparty class, cost, fee, fiat-equivalent reference if any, and evidence reference. Never publish user transaction details.
