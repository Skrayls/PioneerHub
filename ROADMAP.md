## Stage 2 — First revenue (months 3–6)

Only after verified Pi policy/eligibility and user demand: one Mainnet-compatible paid utility with clear standalone value. Goal: first 100 Pi cumulative revenue.

Independent app experiments, beta testing, developer promotion/services, localization. Goal: 1,000 Pi cumulative revenue.

## Gates
