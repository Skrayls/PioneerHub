# Initial project assessment — 2026-08-15

## Verdict

Viable as an education/discovery/community product. It is not yet viable to operate as a marketplace, payment intermediary or revenue business. The highest-confidence first release is a source-backed Lithuanian trust utility.

## Market hypothesis

Lithuania is a practical small-market test bed. The differentiator cannot be referral recruitment or generic news; it must be safer explanations, local language, transparent testing and practical tools.

## Competitive categories

- Official Pi surfaces: authoritative for protocol/account guidance, not localized independent curation.
- Social groups: high reach but fragmented trust and inconsistent moderation.
- Pi app directories: discovery breadth but less local-language explanation and independent review.

## Evidence to collect before investment

Search demand for specific problems, number/quality of active LT communities, user interviews, article completion, app-click behaviour, repeat visits and moderator burden. Do not infer a market from member counts alone.
