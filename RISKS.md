# Risk register

| Risk | Level | Mitigation | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pi trademark/affiliation confusion | High | Independent disclaimer; no Pi in owned brand/domain; obtain licence before any such use. | Brand/public launch |
| User phishing or passphrase loss | High | Safety-first UX; never request/handle passphrases; only official links. | Always |
| MiCA/CASP scope creep | High | No custody, exchange, brokerage, transfers for others, or fiat conversion. | Legal review before scope change |
| GDPR/UGC obligations | Medium | No accounts/data at MVP; data minimization; introduce notices, deletion and moderation before accounts/UGC. | Feature launch |
| Empty marketplace/community | Medium | No marketplace; use practical content and tools first. | Stage 1 evidence |
| Platform dependency | Medium | Own code, content, domain (after approval), analytics and exportable data. | Ongoing |
| Paid-app trust conflict | Medium | Separate editorial scoring from sponsorship; label paid exposure. | App Radar launch |
