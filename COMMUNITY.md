# Community operating model

## Positioning

Independent Lithuanian community for practical Pi participation. It is not Pi Network and is not endorsed by it.

## Acquisition

Give value first: source-backed short guides, scam alerts, app reviews and Testnet demonstrations. Do not spam, scrape groups or pressure users to leave existing communities.

## Referral disclosure

People who already have an inviter should use that inviter. New users may voluntarily use PioneerHub's code when published. No paid recruitment, team transfers, promises of earnings or referral marketplace.
